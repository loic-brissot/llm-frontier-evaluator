Physiological Models

Banister / PMC: CTLₜ = CTLₜ₋₁ + (TSSₜ−CTLₜ₋₁)/42 · ATLₜ = ATLₜ₋₁ + (TSSₜ−ATLₜ₋₁)/7 · TSB = CTL−ATL
Coggan: IF = NP/FTP · TSS = (sec × NP × IF)/(FTP × 3600) × 100 ≈ hours × IF² × 100
Daniels: VDOT from race velocity & duration-adjusted %VO₂max; E/M/T/I/R paces solved from the VO₂–velocity equation.
CSS: CSS = 200/(T₄₀₀−T₂₀₀) m/s · sTSS = IF³ × hours × 100 (cubic swim stress). Strength ≈ 40–50 TSS/h.
