Build an interactive European energy security observatory and supply-shock stress-test simulator crossing energy import dependency, simplified energy balances, renewable shares, household electricity prices, and greenhouse gas emission trajectories across the European Union.
The goal is an exceptional visual result, as insightful and rigorous as a Joint Research Centre (JRC) energy security control room, not a mere technical demo.
Expected result:

* A bivariate and single-metric map of Europe crosses net energy import dependency (%), fossil fuel vs. renewable share in gross available energy, household electricity prices (EUR/kWh), and greenhouse gas emission index (1990 = 100) across all EU-27 member states and EEA neighbors.
* An interactive Energy Balance & Flow Explorer breaks down any selected country's simplified energy balance (primary production `PPRD`, imports `IMP`, exports `EXP`, gross available energy `GAE`, and final energy consumption `FC_E` in GWh) across fuel families (`siec`).
* A live Geopolitical Supply-Shock & Electrification Simulator lets the viewer apply simultaneous stress parameters:
  - Cut external natural gas or oil imports by 0% to 100%,
  - Scale domestic renewable electricity and heat output (+0% to +150%),
  - Shift residential heating and transport demand to electricity (0% to 50% electrification),
  and dynamically recalculates each country's primary energy shortfall (GWh/TWh), self-sufficiency ratio, estimated retail price impact, and CO2 reduction.
* A linked vulnerability scatter matrix plots Energy Import Dependency (x-axis) against Electricity Price or GHG Emissions Index (y-axis), sized by gross available energy (GWh), with interactive quadrant filtering.

Available data sources (European Commission Open Data / CC BY 4.0):

* Eurostat, "Energy imports dependency (nrg_ind_id)" (import dependency % by fuel `siec` and country `geo`): https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/api/dissemination/sdmx/2.1/data/nrg_ind_id?format=SDMX-CSV
* Eurostat, "Simplified energy balances in GWh (nrg_bal_s)" (`PPRD`, `IMP`, `EXP`, `GAE`, `FC_E` from 2018 onward): https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/api/dissemination/sdmx/2.1/data/nrg_bal_s/.PPRD+IMP+EXP+GAE+FC_E..GWH.?format=SDMX-CSV&startPeriod=2018
* Eurostat, "Share of energy from renewable sources (nrg_ind_ren)": https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/api/dissemination/sdmx/2.1/data/nrg_ind_ren?format=SDMX-CSV
* Eurostat, "Electricity prices for household consumers (nrg_pc_204)" (EUR/kWh including taxes, medium consumption band): https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/api/dissemination/sdmx/2.1/data/nrg_pc_204/..KWH2500-4999.KWH.I_TAX.EUR.?format=SDMX-CSV&startPeriod=2015
* Eurostat, "Net greenhouse gas emissions (sdg_13_10)" (index 1990=100 and tonnes per capita): https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/api/dissemination/sdmx/2.1/data/sdg_13_10?format=SDMX-CSV
* GISCO (European Commission), "Countries 2024 — 1:20M GeoJSON boundaries": https://gisco-services.ec.europa.eu/distribution/v2/countries/geojson/CNTR_RG_20M_2024_4326.geojson
