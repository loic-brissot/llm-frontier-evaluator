Build an interactive atlas and progress tracker of renewable energy adoption across the European Union and European Economic Area from 2004 to the present.
The goal is an exceptional visual result, as clear, authoritative and engaging as a European Commission policy observatory, not a mere technical demo.
Expected result:

* An interactive choropleth map of Europe colors all EU-27 and reporting EEA/candidate countries by their share of renewable energy in gross final energy consumption, with a sector switcher across Overall Renewable Share (REN), Renewable Electricity (REN_ELC), Renewable Heating and Cooling (REN_HEAT_CL), and Renewable Transport (REN_TRA).
* A timeline scrubber (2004 to latest validated year) animates national trajectories and allows toggling between absolute share (%) and multi-year percentage-point growth.
* A 2030 EU Target Scorecard ranks countries against the binding EU-wide 42.5% renewable target, computing each country's current gap and required compound annual growth rate (CAGR) to reach 42.5% by 2030.
* A linked multi-country comparison chart lets the viewer select 2 to 6 countries and compare their four-sector profiles side by side over time.
* Clicking any country opens a detailed national energy card showing its historical time series across all four sectors, best- and worst-performing sector, rank within the EU-27, and deviation from the EU-27 average.

Available data sources (European Commission Open Data / CC BY 4.0):

* Eurostat, "Share of energy from renewable sources (nrg_ind_ren)" (annual share by country and energy sector `nrg_bal`: `REN`, `REN_ELC`, `REN_HEAT_CL`, `REN_TRA`):
  - SDMX-CSV bulk endpoint: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/api/dissemination/sdmx/2.1/data/nrg_ind_ren?format=SDMX-CSV
  - JSON-stat API: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/api/dissemination/statistics/1.0/data/nrg_ind_ren?format=JSON&lang=EN
* GISCO (European Commission), "Countries 2024 — 1:20M GeoJSON boundaries": https://gisco-services.ec.europa.eu/distribution/v2/countries/geojson/CNTR_RG_20M_2024_4326.geojson
