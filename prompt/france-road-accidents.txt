Build an interactive map and collision observatory of road accidents in metropolitan France that reveals who hits whom, where, and under what conditions.
The goal is an exceptional visual result, as clear, insightful and impressive as a flagship data-journalism piece or a great atlas, not a mere technical demo.
Expected result:

* Every geolocated corporeal accident in metropolitan France from the latest validated year (or last 3 years) is mapped and colored by worst injury severity (unharmed, slight injury, hospitalized, killed) or by victim transport mode, joining the four BAAC tables (`caracteristiques`, `lieux`, `vehicules`, `usagers`).
* An interactive "who hits whom" collision matrix cross-tabulates the victim's mode of transport (pedestrian, bicycle, e-scooter/EDPM, motorized two-wheeler, passenger car, heavy vehicle) against the opposing vehicle category in the same accident, including single-vehicle crashes and pedestrians struck by a vehicle. Clicking a cell filters the map and all charts.
* Interactive filters slice accidents by hour of day, day of week, lighting conditions, weather, road category, speed limit, and presence of cycling infrastructure.
* A live summary panel updates with the current map viewport and active filters to show total accidents, fatality and hospitalization rates, victim age distribution, and top collision pairs.
* Selecting an individual accident or local cluster inspects its full relational record: location and road profile, involved vehicles, and every user's role, age, sex, safety equipment, and injury outcome.
* Department boundaries provide geographic context across metropolitan France, with national-to-street zoom and a department ranking by accident rate or severity.

Available data sources (Open Licence / Etalab):

* ONISR, "Bases de données annuelles des accidents corporels de la circulation routière (BAAC)" (`caracteristiques`, `lieux`, `vehicules`, `usagers` CSV tables and codebook): https://www.data.gouv.fr/datasets/bases-de-donnees-annuelles-des-accidents-corporels-de-la-circulation-routiere-annees-de-2005-a-2024
* Etalab, "Contours administratifs" (department boundaries GeoJSON): https://etalab-datasets.geo.data.gouv.fr/contours-administratifs/latest/geojson/departements-100m.geojson
* API Découpage administratif (departments and communes lookup): https://geo.api.gouv.fr/departements
