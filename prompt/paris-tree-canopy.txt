Build an interactive map and biodiversity explorer of the municipal tree canopy of Paris.
The goal is an exceptional visual result, as clear, elegant and engaging as an environmental atlas or urban forestry dashboard, not a mere technical demo.
Expected result:

* An interactive map of Paris displays municipal trees with smooth spatial aggregation or clustering at high zooms and individual tree markers at close zooms, color-coded by botanical family/genus or height, with prominent visual badges for remarkable heritage trees (arbres remarquables).
* Interactive filters slice trees by arrondissement (1er to 20e), botanical genus/species (e.g., Platanus, Aesculus, Tilia, Sophora, Celtis), tree height and circumference ranges, and a one-click toggle for remarkable trees.
* An urban forest analytics panel provides summary metrics: total tree count, species and genus diversity breakdown (top species distribution chart), mean and median height/circumference, and canopy density by arrondissement.
* A remarkable trees showcase highlights Paris's heritage trees with their common name, scientific name, estimated planting date or age, remarkable criteria, and direct pan-to-map inspection.
* A district comparison view compares tree coverage across arrondissements, contrasting street trees against parks and public squares, with species variety indicators.
* Selecting any tree opens a detail card showing its exact location, French common name, botanical genus/species, height, circumference, and stage of development.

Available data sources (ODbL / Open Licence):

* Paris Open Data, "Les arbres" (municipal tree inventory with botanical name, height, circumference, address and coordinates): https://opendata.paris.fr/explore/dataset/les-arbres
* Paris Open Data, "Arbres remarquables" (heritage and remarkable trees of Paris with botanical data and history): https://opendata.paris.fr/explore/dataset/arbresremarquablesparis
* Paris Open Data, "Arrondissements" (Paris 20 administrative arrondissements boundaries): https://opendata.paris.fr/explore/dataset/arrondissements
