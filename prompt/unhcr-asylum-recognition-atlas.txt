Build an interactive global asylum-decision, demographic pyramid, and host-responsibility observatory joining UNHCR population stocks, asylum adjudication outcomes, durable solutions, demographic cohorts, and World Bank macroeconomic indicators.
The goal is an exceptional visual result, as rigorous, clear and revealing as an international migration policy research atlas, not a mere technical demo.
Expected result:

* A Host Responsibility & Burden Index map and scatter matrix cross each host country's displaced population against its national population (displaced per 1,000 inhabitants) and economic capacity (displaced per $1B GDP), contrasting low- and middle-income frontline hosts against high-income economies.
* An interactive Origin x Destination Asylum Recognition Matrix cross-tabulates major origin nationalities (`coo_iso`) against destination asylum jurisdictions (`coa_iso`), comparing total decisions (`dec_total`), Geneva Convention recognition rate (`dec_recognized`), complementary protection rate (`dec_other`), and rejection rate (`dec_rejected`) for the exact same origin group across different host countries.
* A Demographic Cohort Pyramid panel renders the age (`0–4`, `5–11`, `12–17`, `18–59`, `60+`) and sex (`f_*` vs. `m_*`) breakdown of displaced populations for any selected country, highlighting the share of children (<18) and elderly dependents.
* A Durable Solutions & Net Protection Balance view compares displaced stocks against voluntary repatriation returns (`returned_refugees`, `returned_idps`), third-country resettlement (`resettlement`), and local naturalization (`naturalisation`) from 2018 to 2024.

Available data sources (Open Data — UNHCR & World Bank, no API key required):

* UNHCR Bilateral Population Stocks (2024): https://api.unhcr.org/population/v1/population/?limit=10000&year=2024&coo_all=true&coa_all=true
* UNHCR Bilateral Asylum Decisions (`dec_recognized`, `dec_other`, `dec_rejected`, `dec_closed`, `dec_total`): https://api.unhcr.org/population/v1/asylum-decisions/?limit=10000&year=2024&coo_all=true&coa_all=true
* UNHCR Durable Solutions (`returned_refugees`, `resettlement`, `naturalisation`, `returned_idps`, 2018–2024): https://api.unhcr.org/population/v1/solutions/?limit=10000&yearFrom=2018&yearTo=2024&coo_all=true&coa_all=true
* UNHCR Displaced Demographics by Age & Sex (`f_0_4`, `m_0_4`, `f_5_11`, `m_5_11`, `f_12_17`, `m_12_17`, `f_18_59`, `m_18_59`, `f_60`, `m_60`): https://api.unhcr.org/population/v1/demographics/?limit=10000&year=2024&coa_all=true
* World Bank Total Population (`SP.POP.TOTL`) & GDP (`NY.GDP.MKTP.CD`):
  - https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/all/indicator/SP.POP.TOTL?format=json&per_page=1000&date=2023
  - https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/all/indicator/NY.GDP.MKTP.CD?format=json&per_page=1000&date=2023
* GISCO World Countries 2024 GeoJSON boundaries: https://gisco-services.ec.europa.eu/distribution/v2/countries/geojson/CNTR_RG_20M_2024_4326.geojson
