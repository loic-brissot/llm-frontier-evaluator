    Atlas ferroviaire

    A 24-hour replay of the scheduled French passenger rail timetable — TGV (INOUI, OUIGO, ICE, Lyria), Intercités and TER — with travel-time accessibility and daily corridor flows.

    How it is computed

    • Trains move along the real track: every stop-to-stop leg of every trip is routed over the RFN line geometry (shortest path; TGV prefers high-speed lines, TER/Intercités avoid them). Legs without track data (abroad) are drawn straight. Positions are interpolated between the scheduled times — no delays.
    • Accessibility runs an earliest-arrival Connection Scan over the day's timetable (≥ 5 min to change trains, walking links under 650 m), departing at the timeline clock. Travel time counts from the first train and includes every transfer wait.
    • Corridor flow counts the scheduled trains crossing each section of the network over the service day.
    • Service days shown: Tue 17, Sat 14 and Sun 15 November 2026. Trips running past midnight wrap onto the next morning.
