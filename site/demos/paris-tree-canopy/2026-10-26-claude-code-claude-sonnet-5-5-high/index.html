About the data

220,108 trees of the municipal inventory “Les arbres”, the 183 records (182 distinct trees) of “Arbres remarquables” and the 20 arrondissement boundaries come from Paris Open Data (Ville de Paris, ODbL / Licence Ouverte).

  • Heights above 60 m and girths above 10 m are treated as data-entry errors; zeros mean “not recorded”.
  • The inventory also covers city-owned trees in the Bois de Boulogne, the Bois de Vincennes and a few suburban parks and cemeteries — they are kept as separate groups, not mixed into the 20 arrondissements.
  • “Density” is trees per hectare of arrondissement area — not a measured canopy cover. The 12e and 16e outlines include the Bois de Vincennes and Boulogne, so their area is counted without the Bois (995 ha and 846 ha).
  • Planting dates are only known for 77 heritage trees; for the others, age is a rough estimate from girth.
